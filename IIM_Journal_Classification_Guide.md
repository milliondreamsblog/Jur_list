## 1. Executive Summary: The 2026 IIM Journal Evaluation Ecosystem

In 2026, Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs) evaluate academic research using a unified benchmark consisting of:
1. **ABDC Quality List** (Australian Business Deans Council) – *The primary research quality standard (A\*, A, B, C)*
2. **FT50 (Financial Times Top 50)** – *The elite standard used to calculate FT Global B-School research ranks*
3. **UTD 24 (UT Dallas 24)** – *The premier 24 journals tracked for foundational academic rigor*
4. **Scopus (Elsevier)** – *Global abstract & citation database (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 quartiles)*
5. **Web of Science / Clarivate (SSCI / SCIE / ESCI)** – *Journal Impact Factor & citation ranking*
6. **ABS / CABS Academic Journal Guide (AJG)** – *4\*, 4, 3, 2, 1 ratings*
7. **UGC-CARE (India)** – *Statutory regulatory compliance (Group II automatically includes Scopus & WoS)*

## 5. Master Database Download & Verification Portals

1. **ABDC Master Search & Download Portal:**
   * Direct Link: [https://abdc.edu.au/research/abdc-journal-list/](https://abdc.edu.au/research/abdc-journal-list/)
   * Allows downloading the complete official Excel dataset covering ~2,700 management journals.
2. **Scopus Source Title List (Elsevier):**
   * Direct Link: [https://www.scopus.com/sources.uri](https://www.scopus.com/sources.uri)
   * Elsevier releases an updated source list Excel spreadsheet containing CiteScore, SNIP, and SJR for all active journals.
3. **SCImago Journal & Country Rank (SJR):**
   * Direct Link: [https://www.scimagojr.com](https://www.scimagojr.com)
   * Filter specifically for *Business, Management and Accounting* and view live Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4 quartiles.
4. **Clarivate Web of Science Master Journal List:**
   * Direct Link: [https://mjl.clarivate.com](https://mjl.clarivate.com)
   * Verify SSCI (Social Sciences Citation Index), SCIE, and ESCI status.
5. **UGC-CARE (India) University Grants Commission Portal:**
   * Direct Link: [https://ugccare.unipune.ac.in](https://ugccare.unipune.ac.in)
   * Search Group I (UGC scrutinized journals) and Group II (Scopus/WoS indexed journals).
