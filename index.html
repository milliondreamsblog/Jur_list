ABDC Quality Framework

Australian Business Deans Council ranks journals as A* (Top 5-7%), A, B, and C. IIMs heavily weight A* and A publications for faculty appointments, tenures, and thesis graduation.

FT 50 & UTD 24 Elite Standard

Financial Times 50 and UT Dallas 24 represent the highest research prestige. Publications in these journals boost institutional international ranks and earn premier research incentives across IIMs.

Scopus & UGC-CARE Group II

Scopus divides journals into Quartiles (Q1 to Q4). Under Indian UGC guidelines, any journal indexed in Scopus or Web of Science is automatically approved under UGC-CARE Group II.
